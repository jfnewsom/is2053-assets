Unit Cadence

Fall 2026 runs as a full 16-week term from Wed Aug 19 through Fri Dec 11. Each module is three weeks. Modules 1–4 follow the same cadence: two BookEx exercises, two BUILD/ENHANCE labs, and a MASTER capstone, with a short multiple-choice exam that opens at the end of Week 2 and closes early in Week 3—so it sits inside its own module and never competes with the capstone. Module 5 (Chapter 10 only) is a single BookEx and two labs (BUILD + MASTER), with no Lab 5.3, and its exam runs in the finals period. Fall Break (Oct 12–18) and Thanksgiving (Nov 23–29) are full weeks off with nothing due.

How Module 5 Differs

Module 5 is the only module that does not run the full BUILD/ENHANCE/MASTER three-lab cadence. Chapter 11 (Inheritance) was dropped from the course, so there is no Lab 5.3. What remains is Chapter 10 (Classes and Objects) split across two labs:

  • Lab 5.1 (BUILD) — You build the City class from scratch and refactor your Module 4 game to use City objects.
  • Lab 5.2 (MASTER) — You build the Player class against a provided lab-5-2.py, completing the full object-oriented version of the game.

Lab 5.1 (BUILD) keeps the same Sunday deadline as every other Lab X.1 (Sun Nov 22). After the Thanksgiving break week (Nov 23–29), Lab 5.2 (MASTER) is due Sun Dec 6, and the Module 5 exam opens that same day and closes Tue Dec 8 in the finals period — the Monday–Tuesday window means no one has to do both on Sunday.

University Holidays
  • Mon Sep 7 — Labor Day (university closed)
  • Oct 12 – 13 — Fall Break (Mon & Tue) — no classes; normal Friday/Sunday due dates for the week are unaffected
  • Nov 23 – 29 — Thanksgiving week — no deliverables

Thanksgiving is a genuine no-work week — nothing is due. Fall Break is Monday and Tuesday only (Oct 12–13). Nothing is due on the break days, and the week keeps its normal Friday/Sunday deadlines. The in-person section misses its Tuesday meeting that week; Thursday meets as usual. Labor Day needs no accommodation; it falls on an off-day with nothing due. Every module unlocks in full at the start of its Week 1, so you may work ahead through a break if you like.

All Due Times Are 11:59 p.m. US Central Time

BookEx (Friday) and Lab (Sunday) deadlines are 11:59 p.m. US Central Time (the home time zone of UT San Antonio) on their due date, no matter where you are located. Module exams open Sunday at the end of Week 2 and close the following Tuesday at the same 11:59 p.m. (Module 3 shifts to Monday–Wednesday for Fall Break; Module 5 runs Sun Dec 6–Tue Dec 8.) Canvas enforces these cutoffs automatically, so submit early to be safe.
