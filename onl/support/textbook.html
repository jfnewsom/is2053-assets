The Book You Need

Starting Out with Python, 6th Edition by Tony Gaddis (Pearson)
ISBN: 9780137871209

You do not have to buy it at the UT San Antonio Bookstore or pay full price on Amazon. The textbook is widely available through rentals, ebook subscriptions, used copies, and library systems. Shop around and pick the format and price that fit you.

schedule
Get It Early

Chapter 2 reading and BookEx exercises start in Week 1, and BookEx 0 has you type a program straight from the book. Don’t wait until you’re behind to track down a copy.

Where to Look
  • UT San Antonio Library: check if a copy is on course reserve. Free beats every other option.
  • Interlibrary Loan (ILL): UT San Antonio can borrow from other libraries when ours doesn’t have it.
  • Pearson direct: eTextbook subscription or rental straight from the publisher.
  • Ebook rentals: VitalSource, RedShelf, and Chegg often beat purchase prices for a one-semester rental.
  • Used copies: AbeBooks, ThriftBooks, eBay, Half Price Books, and Bookfinder.com regularly have it well under retail.
  • International editions: same content as the US edition, frequently a fraction of the price. Worth a search.
  • UT San Antonio Bookstore and Amazon: still valid options if you want the convenience.

The Course Materials section of the syllabus lists the eTextbook and Kindle options, both available immediately.

lightbulb
5th Edition Works Too

If you find a 5th edition cheap, it covers almost everything we do. A handful of program numbers shifted between editions (mostly in Chapters 4–6). The course’s BookEx pages flag the differences so you won’t get lost.

Print vs. Digital

Physical books are easier to annotate and flip through. Ebooks are searchable and travel well. Pick what fits how you actually study. Both work.
